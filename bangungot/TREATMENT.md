# Bangungot: treatment

Dark stickman horror reel, 1080×1920, 24 fps, about 68 s, Tagalog narrator (Angelo), word-by-word captions.

**Hook:** frame 1 is two eyes snapping open under a red 3:00 AM, with a boom and a heartbeat. No intro.

**Story (0–23 s):** you can't move, you can't scream. Something in the corner. It comes closer, one jump per word. It sits on your chest.
**Choice (17–25 s):** A: shut your eyes. B: look at it. 3-2-1, "Comment A o B".
**Scare (~23 s):** "Kahit ano'ng piliin mo…", silence, then the face, full frame, with a scream.
**Twist (25–60 s):** "Hindi siya multo." SLEEP PARALYSIS. During REM sleep the brain switches the body off; sometimes the mind wakes first and the dream leaks into the room. The same shadow is seen everywhere: batibat, kanashibari, Old Hag. About 8 in 100 people have had it. What to do: don't fight it, wiggle your toes, breathe slowly. It passes.
**End (60–68 s):** "silipin mo muna ang sulok" — the corner, a flicker, it's there. "Comment OO". The eyes open again, so the reel loops.

**Editing rules:** no dead air (narrator lines back to back, internal pauses capped at 0.3 s), a cut or a hit on every line, key words in red, the music ducks under the voice.
