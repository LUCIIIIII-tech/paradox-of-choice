# Mag-isa (The Last Human): treatment

Stickman "scenario" reel, 1080×1920, 24 fps, about 1:33, Tagalog narrator (Angelo), word-by-word captions, same series look as Bangungot.

**Hook (0–2 s):** frame 1 is a busy crosswalk full of people. On "wala", everyone turns to dust at once, with a flash and a BRAAM, and their phones clatter onto the road. Only you are left, in a red glow.
**Open loop (2–7 s):** "Panoorin hanggang dulo. Ang unang sisira sa'yo… hindi gutom." The answer is held back until about 0:55.
**Time counter (retention):** ARAW 1 → ORAS 1 → ORAS 24 → ARAW 2 → ARAW 3 → LINGGO 1 → BUWAN 1 → TAON 1 → 10 → 100 → 10,000. Every jump gets a riser and a hit.
**Escalation:** empty street, freedom on the highway, the power plants shut down, the city goes dark block by block, Earth goes dark, the subway floods, no signal, dogs hunting in packs, talking to yourself on a rooftop, "may sumagot", then the scare.
**Twist (psychology):** the voice is your brain. In a sensory-deprivation study, 15 minutes in total darkness was enough for people to see faces. When there is no one, the brain makes people. What breaks you first: not hunger, loneliness.
**Payoff:** the world takes itself back (grass, deer, rust, a forest city, almost no trace after 10,000 years). You are the last one who remembers us.
**Ending:** "Hindi tayo kailangan ng mundo. Pero kailangan natin ang isa't isa." Message someone you miss; comment the first thing you'd do. The crowd flickers back in, so the reel loops to frame 1.
