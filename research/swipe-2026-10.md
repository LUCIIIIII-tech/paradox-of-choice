## What the two analysed reels do
- Line one starts at 0.0 s, over moving picture, with a big title on screen in frame 1.
- **A (simulif):** a continuous 3D camera (2 cuts in 105 s), with the change coming from the DAY counter and stat HUD instead. Each beat is one sourced number with its source tag (WHO, World Bank, "my math"). It ends on a twist that reframes the topic ("For half the world, this was never a what-if") and then the first words again ("Day one"), so it loops. The CTA asks what you'd change, plus an "easter egg" prompt that drives rewatches.
- **B (Stickocalypse):** a cut every ~1.5 s (40 cuts in 61 s). Captions are uppercase, 2–4 words, with one coloured key word. Eli and his dog are a named hero pair, and a "FARM RULE #2" card plus a supplies HUD give it a game feel. It ends on a cliffhanger, "Someone was already inside the fence", with a PART 2 title card. 171K followers from 5 posts shows the series format working.
- Both sit at −14 LUFS, and voice is never under the music.

## Apply to Dark Psyche
1. Frame 1 gets a big title: "What if EVERYONE disappeared?" with one red word, over the moving hook.
2. A stat HUD for each fact: number, label, source tag.
3. A named recurring stickman (+ pet) across episodes; series with PART 2 cliffhangers.
4. A cut or insert every 1.5–2 s, not once per line.
5. An easter egg in every reel ("Did you spot the shadow at 0:23?") for rewatches.
6. Topics: universal, everyday, one-sentence what-ifs. Psychology angles: never sleeping again, hearing everyone's thoughts, losing fear, forgetting everyone you love, the last sound you'd hear.
