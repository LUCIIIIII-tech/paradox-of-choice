# Spotlight Effect: treatment

Art Deco reel, 1080×1920 (9:16), 24 fps, 90 s. Tagalog on-screen text (deco subtitle cards, plus a `.srt`), no voice. Original symphonic-jazz score and foley, synthesised in code.

Topic: **the spotlight effect** (Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky, 2000): we overestimate how much other people notice us. Chosen because almost everyone has replayed an embarrassing moment at 3 a.m.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. The stage that wasn't watched (chosen).** Mara trips on a ballroom stair in front of "everyone". Her imagination lights a ring of staring eyes and a bulb marquee that spells NAKAKAHIYA. At 3 a.m. she is still replaying it. The doors split open on the real crowd: every guest stands under their own little spotlight, busy with their own worry. Then the number: the T-shirt study's guess was about half, the real figure 23 %. In a Busby Berkeley overhead, everyone is the star of their own circle. Mara gets up and finishes the dance.
- The spotlight is literal, so the style's light, ornament and counting all serve the idea.

**B. The world's fair.** Five pavilions, one idea of the effect behind each door. It's tidy, but it's a lecture with no protagonist and no feeling.

**C. The newsreel.** A period announcer explains the study over archival-style cards. This needs a voice, and there is no Tagalog voice engine in this build. Rejected.

## 2. Arc

- **Hook (0–6 s)**: spotlight slams on, Mara trips, champagne glasses fly. "Nadapa ka. Sa harap ng LAHAT."
- **The feeling (6–24 s)**: staring eyes switch on one per beat; NAKAKAHIYA lights letter by letter; a clock spins to 3 a.m. while she stares awake.
- **The reveal (24–44 s)**: "PERO ANG TOTOO…" The doors split onto the crowd, each guest in their own spotlight with a mirror, a stain, a watch or a speech card. Thought medallions show each person's own worry.
- **The science (44–68 s)**: title bar SPOTLIGHT EFFECT. The study: an embarrassing T-shirt, Cornell 2000. A dial rises to "HULA NILA ≈ 50 %", then drops with a clang to "ANG TOTOO: 23 %", about 1 in 4 people.
- **The lesson (68–90 s)**: a mirrored overhead kaleidoscope of spotlights, each one someone's own story. Mara stands, bows and dances. The lights go out until one bulb sign is left: SPOTLIGHT EFFECT, then the share line.

## 3. Look

- Warm lacquer black, banded gold line, ivory. **Mara's burgundy is the only saturated colour.** The spotlight is warm ivory light.
- Centre axis in every shot. Act changes are door leaves splitting on the seam.
- Characters step on twos (12 fps). Camera, light and bulbs run on ones.

## 4. Score

Tempo 120 bpm (1 beat = 0.5 s). Every cut and every counted light falls on the beat grid.

| Section | Time | Music |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–6 | crash stinger, low piano cluster, tremolo |
| Feeling | 6–18 | minor tango (D minor): pizzicato bass, piano stabs, a bell per eye and per bulb letter |
| 3 a.m. | 18–24 | **silence**: only a clock tick |
| Reveal | 24–44 | door chord, then a warm foxtrot in F major: walking bass, brushes, clarinet melody |
| Science | 44–68 | sparse piano and pizzicato, a bell glissando on the dial, a bronze clang at 23 % |
| Lesson | 68–90 | full Charleston: piano stride, clarinet, muted brass. Lights out to one piano note and a final hit |

## 5. Text

Josefin Sans SemiBold on stepped lacquer cards that unfold from the centre, sitting at y ≈ 1340–1500, above the phone UI. Titles in Limelight, numbers in Poiret One. Every card holds for at least 2.6 s.

## 6. Fact

Gilovich, T., Medvec, V. H., & Savitsky, K. (2000). *The spotlight effect in social judgment.* Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 78(2), 211–222. Students who wore an embarrassing T-shirt guessed that about half of their classmates would notice. About 23 % actually did.
