# Puyat (what sleep loss does to you): treatment

Look: every scene is drawn in full colour, then darkened; only the lights (lamp amber, screen cyan, moon blue, neon magenta, danger red) bring the colour back. Smooth camera only, no glitch effects.

**Hook (frame 1):** from above, Nico lies wide awake at 3:00, staring straight at you, the title already up: "PAANO KUNG HINDI KA NA MAKATULOG?". The first frame lands on an impact and a braam, and a clock starts ticking faster. A riser cuts dead on "makakatulog", then a hit and a punch-in.
