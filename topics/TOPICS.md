# Topic bank: Tagalog horror + what-if reels

30 topics, 60-90 s each, second-person ("ikaw") like the rest of the channel. Ranked by score.
Topics we already made are left out: aswang, balete, bangungot, katok, huling tren, puyat, mag-isa, blackout, tsunami, El Niño, zombie, presidente, tatlong bansa, hindi namamatay, hear thoughts, CCTV, puntod.

**Score (1-5 each, max 15)**, from the yt-retention rules:
- **H, hook:** does the first 3 s land a threat or an impossible fact? (The hook leak is always the first 15 s of script.)
- **C, cliff safety:** is there one question that holds from the hook to the end, with no long setup and no topic change? 5 = no cliff risk.
- **T, twist:** does the ending flip what we saw, and make people rewatch and comment?

Each entry gives: the hook (said word for word), the engine (the question that keeps them watching), the twist, the comment CTA, and the 3D set.
