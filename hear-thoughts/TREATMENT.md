# What If You Could Hear Everyone's Thoughts?

A 16:9 long-form "what if" story for YouTube (about 8 minutes), English first, then Tagalog. Same narrator (VoxCPM2 clone of
`voice-tests/18_Vox_deep_narrator.mp3`), same dark painterly engine as PUYAT and HULING TREN, smooth camera, no glitches.

## The idea
You wake up and can hear everyone's thoughts until midnight. One day, hour by hour, every scene a relatable moment, and every
scene ends on a real psychology finding. Six questions make it interactive: answer out loud before the timer runs out, keep
score out of four, and comment the answers to the last two. The payoff is one secret that changes how you see yourself:
everyone is too busy worrying about being judged to judge you.

## Retention devices
A question every minute or so, each with a 5-second countdown; open loops ("it matters later", "one secret by midnight"); a time-of-day
card at every hour; bubbles that type each thought as it is heard; a stat card on every finding; a cut or a camera move at least
every few seconds; the payoff ties every earlier scene back together.

## Sound
Thoughts you hear clearly are the narrator made thinner, wider and wet (inside your head); the crowd of thoughts is the narrator's
own lines turned into whispers, panned all around. Per scene foley (kitchen, train, office, café, park, platform, night, rooftop),
a warm felt-piano motif for the human moments, a pulse under each question, clock ticks and a heartbeat under each countdown.

## End screen
The last 12 seconds hold a "watch next" frame (right, top) and a subscribe circle (right, bottom) for YouTube end-screen elements.
