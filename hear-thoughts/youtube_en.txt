What If You Could Hear Everyone's Thoughts for One Day?

Tomorrow morning you wake up and you can hear what everyone is thinking, until midnight. Your mom, your little brother, a packed train, your boss, your best friend, your crush, a stranger on the platform. And by the end of the day you learn one secret about people that changes how you see yourself.
